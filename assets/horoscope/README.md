# AURA horoscope artwork

The seven daily backgrounds are the approved 1080 × 1920 PNGs shared by the horoscope cover and reading story. The palette is peach ivory on Sunday, moon pearl on Monday, brick red on Tuesday, celadon on Wednesday, buttercream on Thursday, rose on Friday, and lavender on Saturday. Each plate includes the bright gold AURA orb and crescent above a pale center for the cover or readings.

`background-none.png` is the original neutral ivory plate used for No tint.

`ivory-emblems.png` is the original artwork used for the gold sign emblems. The shared renderer draws the reading cards and text over the selected background.

`zodiac-cover-ring.png` is the transparent antique gold cover ornament with twelve figures in Aries-to-Pisces order and an open center for the date. The cover renderer places the ring and AURA wordmark over the selected background and draws the localized title, date, and weekday using local fonts. All artwork is stored locally.
